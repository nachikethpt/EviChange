◆ EviChange claim adjudication
Rules (proposed, docs/adjudication_rules.md)
  • Judge only what is inside the box, at the box's dates. The condition and verdict are hidden on purpose.
  • Unverifiable only for what no imagery can show: cause, purpose, legality, ownership, the future. "Mining" is a cause.
  • About the detector: tick it when the claim describes the detector or evidence ("no change was detected") rather than the ground.
  • Area within a factor of 2 of what is visible, share within ±15 points: true. Index values: judge only the direction they imply.
  • Change types: the label is the reference; the chips may add a type it misses, never contradict one it has.
  • Hedges don't change the judgement. A claim with a false part is false; tick compound when it joins statements.
  • Pass 2 (support) is judged against the evidence JSON only, not the chips.

Choose the reports, your name and the pass, then Start.
